# HR Management System 

## Overview
HR Management System is a complete employee and workforce management solution developed using Laravel. The system helps companies manage employees, attendance, payroll, leaves, recruitment, departments, and daily HR operations efficiently through a secure admin dashboard.

---

## Features

### Employee Management
- Add, Edit & Delete Employees
- Employee Profile Management
- Employee ID Generation
- Department & Designation Management
- Employee Document Upload

### Attendance Management
- Daily Attendance Tracking
- Check-In & Check-Out
- Monthly Attendance Reports
- Attendance History

### Leave Management
- Apply Leave Requests
- Leave Approval/Rejection
- Multiple Leave Types
- Leave Balance Tracking

### Payroll Management
- Salary Management
- Monthly Payroll Generation
- Bonus & Deduction Management
- Payslip Download

### Reports & Analytics
- Employee Reports
- Attendance Reports
- Payroll Reports
- Leave Reports

---

## Technology Stack

- Laravel
- PHP
- MySQL
- Bootstrap
- AJAX
- Blade Template
- REST APIs
